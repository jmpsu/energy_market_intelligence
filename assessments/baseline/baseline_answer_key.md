# Baseline answer key

1. Gas and NGLs can be stored and shipped as molecules; electricity must be produced the instant it is used. [K]
2. Growth rate matters as much as level: fast-growing basins outrun their pipelines. [K]
3. The contract is a standard monthly volume in MMBtu for physical delivery at the hub. [K]
4. Supply-push corridors exist because a basin must clear; demand-pull corridors because a market must be fed. [K]
5. GPM = mol% x a component factor (gal per Mcf per mol%). Summed over C2+ it measures how rich (wet) the gas is [K]. Rich gas means more liquids revenue per Mcf processed.
6. Wholesale is generators selling to utilities and retailers; retail is sales to end users. [K]
7. Nuclear runs flat out; hydro is flexible but limited by water. [K]
8. The middle office measures risk, validates curves and reports P&L independently. [K]
9. Returns are usually log price changes. [K]
10. The objective is cost to minimise or margin to maximise. [K]
11. Utilities and IPPs need planning and dispatch tools. [K]
12. When gas is marginal, power price follows gas price times heat rate. [K]
